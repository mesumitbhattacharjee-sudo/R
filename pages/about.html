MANIFESTO

The Antidote to Disposable Fast Fashion

CGAPH was founded on a simple thesis: what you wear should represent your distinct creative intent. Rather than over-producing thousands of garments that sit in warehouses, we build exclusively on-demand.

🔬

Discharge Direct-to-Garment

Traditional screen prints leave a rubbery plastic feel that cracks after five washes. Our Japanese discharge pigment replaces the fabric's original dye at the molecular level, creating a breathably soft hand-feel that outlasts the garment itself.

⚖️

Architectural 260+ GSM Drape

Flimsy 140 GSM cotton hugs unflatteringly and warps in the wash. CGAPH garments are custom-knit from 260 to 450 GSM combed organic cotton, designed to maintain structured, boxy silhouettes with zero side-seam torque.

🌱

Zero Landfill Overproduction

Every single piece is manufactured after you hit "Place Order." By eliminating speculative warehouse inventory, we eradicate deadstock waste while preserving the highest possible standard of craft.
