Policies & Operational Security

Transparent standards governing our bespoke manufacturing, shipping, and data integrity.

1. Global Fulfillment & Shipping

All orders are manufactured within 48 business hours of receipt. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days domestically, and 6-10 days internationally via DHL Express or FedEx. Tracking numbers are generated immediately once the item leaves the direct print node.

2. 100% Quality Print Guarantee

Because every piece is printed bespoke, we cannot accept returns for buyer's remorse. However, if your garment arrives with any printing defect, misaligned graphic, physical tear, or incorrect size, we will issue an immediate complimentary reprint or full refund within 30 days of delivery.

3. Cloud Security & Data Isolation

Customer orders, shipping addresses, and personal credentials are encrypted using Google Cloud Firestore and authenticated via Firebase Authentication. User profiles are isolated with granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensuring only the authenticated document owner or verified store administrators can access private telemetry.
